Who I am

I'm Utsav Ratpiya, a final year B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at Adani University, Ahmedabad (CGPA 8.04). I specialise in building AI-powered systems — from deep learning models to data dashboards.

My hands-on work spans computer vision (MobileNetV2 & YOLOv8 pipelines on 12,000+ images and live video), NLP-powered recommendation & chatbot systems, interactive Power BI & Streamlit dashboards, financial analytics platforms, and end-to-end ML deployment on Hugging Face, Streamlit, Flask, and Render.

Currently exploring Generative AI, AI Agents & Automation. Open to AI/ML internships, research collaborations, and data analytics roles.
